# Vexil Search - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At Vexil Search, our absolute highest priority is the privacy and security of your data. This policy outlines exactly what happens to your data when you use the Vexil Search application.

## 1. 100% Offline Processing Guarantee

Vexil Search is engineered to function as an offline-first desktop application.

- **No Cloud Indexing:** All file indexing, text parsing, and search queries happen strictly locally on your hardware. We do not upload your files, metadata, or search history to any remote server.
- **No Cloud AI:** Any intelligent categorization or machine learning models bundled with Vexil execute entirely within the local memory of your machine.
- **Zero Telemetry:** We do not collect product analytics, usage telemetry, or crash reports in the background.

## 2. What Data We Collect

Because Vexil Search operates locally, we do not collect, store, or have access to any of your personal files.

The **only** time Vexil communicates with our servers is for License Validation purposes (if you purchase the Pro Tier):

- **License Key Activation:** When you enter a Pro license key, Vexil transmits the key and a generated anonymized hardware identifier (Machine Fingerprint) to our licensing server via a secure HTTPS connection.
- **License Heartbeat:** Vexil may periodically check the status of your license key against our servers to ensure it has not been refunded, revoked, or flagged for piracy. This heartbeat only transmits your license key and hardware identifier.

## 3. Data Protection

Because your files never leave your computer, the security of your indexed data is bound strictly to the security of your operating system. Vexil Search stores its high-speed index databases locally in your system's application data directory. You are responsible for employing full-disk encryption (such as BitLocker or FileVault) and standard OS-level security measures to protect this local index from unauthorized physical or remote access.

## 4. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy at any time. If we introduce new online features (such as an optional cloud backup feature for settings), we will explicitly update this document and require an opt-in before data is transmitted.

## 5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official repository or website.
