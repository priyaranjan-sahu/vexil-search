Why Vexil?

Built from the ground up for speed, security, and developer productivity.

⚡

Blazing Fast Rust Core

The backend is written entirely in Rust, utilizing a custom Tauri architecture to scan and index files at native speeds with minimal memory footprint.

🔒

100% Local & Private

Your data is yours. Vexil indexes everything directly on your machine. No telemetry, no cloud syncing, and absolutely no code leaves your hard drive.

🧠

Smart AI Search

Stop relying on exact keyword matches. Vexil understands context, intelligently categorizing your codebase and documents to surface what matters.
